Caption. International students sit at the center of this network, holding six of the ten relationships. Their riskiest link is with landlords, where deliberate scams and rushed, time-pressured decisions both occur. Scam risk also travels through peer channels, when fake sublets and listings circulate in student groups. Harvard housing timing adds pressure that pushes students into the private market. Some MC/MPA students are also international, so they share these risks.

Scams

Deliberate deception: someone sets out to take the student's money.

    What helps: warnings and verification, such as confirming who owns a unit and never paying before viewing.

    Rushed decisions

    Time pressure, not fraud: deadlines, distance and missing information lead to poor choices.

      What helps: earlier information, local pricing benchmarks, and backup temporary housing.

      Gap to note: no group on the map links to landlords in a way that could hold them accountable or warn students about them.
