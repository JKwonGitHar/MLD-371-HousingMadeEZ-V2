Housing Made EZ · Network summary

Student Housing Network

International graduate students sit at the center, and most of their search runs through the private market, where nothing screens for scams. Both of their landlords, private landlords and Harvard University Housing, are strained relationships. Day-to-day advice comes from peers, especially HKS international student groups and MC/MPA classmates. Every student also faces six move-in hurdles, and only one, spotting scams, has any help on the map today. The dashed circles on the right are the groups to research next.

Open questions to confirm

  • Do students see the City of Boston's scam guidance before they sign, or only after something goes wrong?
  • Are international students eligible for Division of Housing Stabilization emergency help?
  • Does reporting a scam to police lead to any housing outcome, or only a record?
  • How are students told about GSAS housing, if at all, and how often is space available?
  • Does the Harvard International Office's housing guidance reach students before they start searching?
