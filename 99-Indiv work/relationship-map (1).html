Where international students go for housing help

International students are at the center because they are the ones seeking housing help. MC/MPA students and HKS international student groups are subgroups of that community, shown inside the shaded area, and students turn to both for day-to-day advice. Students deal with two landlords, private landlords and Harvard housing, and hear from the Harvard Graduate Council by email. Both landlord relationships are strained, with private landlords and with Harvard housing, and are shown in red. That advice comes daily through WhatsApp and face to face. Circles are sized by how many relationships each group holds, lines are labeled by channel, and thicker lines mean more frequent contact.

Draft: channel, frequency and strain have not been specified yet for of the connections. Those are drawn as gray dashed lines until the values are filled in (see the RELATIONSHIPS block in this file's source).

Line thickness shows the team's estimate of how often each group is in contact, not a measured figure. It will be updated with survey results.
