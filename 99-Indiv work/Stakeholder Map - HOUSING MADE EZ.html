Stakeholder relationship map for international student housing

This map expands the original sketch beyond students, peer groups, and landlords. It shows the actors that shape a student’s housing journey, the role each one plays, and where the first MVP should build, refer, or simply observe.

Recommended MVP boundary

Build between international students, Harvard guidance channels, and the private rental market. The first product should help students find a trustworthy next step, evaluate a listing, prepare for an application, and escalate questions safely.

Build with

Students, current-student peer networks, HIO, school offices, HUH, and the Harvard off-campus housing platform.

Refer or verify

Landlords, brokers, city housing offices, inspection services, legal aid, fair-housing resources, and formal tenant-rights guidance.

What this changes from the first draft

The original map was a useful interpersonal sketch, but it made the ecosystem look like students asking two kinds of landlords for help. This version separates housing supply, Harvard gateways, public safeguards, and neighborhood context. It also removes unvalidated frequency and “strained” labels. Those are research questions, not established facts.

The map therefore points toward a focused MVP: a trusted navigation layer over existing resources, not an all-in-one housing marketplace.
