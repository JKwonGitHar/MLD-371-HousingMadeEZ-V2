Keep it safe

  • Never post passport, visa or ID numbers, bank or card details, or your address.
  • Do not share emails or phone numbers in public posts.
  • If you were scammed, contact your bank, then report it. See the Massachusetts renter basics on the main site.
  • Posts here are experiences, not legal advice.
