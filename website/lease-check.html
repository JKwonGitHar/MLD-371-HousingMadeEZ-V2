Three golden rules, whatever the lease says

  1. See it before you pay. In person, or a live video call with someone inside.
  2. Never wire money or pay with gift cards or crypto. You can’t get it back.
  3. Upfront, the most you owe is first month + last month + a deposit up to one month + a new lock.
